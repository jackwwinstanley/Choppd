Privacy Policy

Effective 18 July 2026

Choppd is a cooking app. We keep this short and honest: we collect only what the app needs to work, we don't run ads, and we don't sell your data.

What we collect

  • Your email address — for passwordless sign-in. We send you a one-time code; there are no passwords.
  • Cook activity — the cooks you complete, your ratings, and any feedback you send. This is how the app tracks your progress and improves the recipes.
  • Camera — only when you use the fridge scan. A photo of your ingredients is sent securely, used only to identify what you have, and immediately discarded. We never store your photos, and our provider processes them solely to identify ingredients — never for advertising or training.
  • Technical basics — your IP address and device type show up in our routine server logs. We use them only to keep Choppd secure and working.

How we use it

To run your account, remember your cooks, match recipes to your ingredients, and make the app better. That's it.

How long we keep it

  • Account and cook data — kept while your account is active, and removed within 30 days after you delete it.
  • Sign-in codes — expire in a few minutes and aren't kept.
  • Fridge-scan photos — processed and immediately discarded. They're never stored.
  • Server logs — kept up to 90 days to keep Choppd secure, then deleted.

What we don't do

  • No ads.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We don't send marketing emails — email is for your sign-in codes and support only.
  • No sharing with third parties beyond the service providers we need to run Choppd: Resend (sends your sign-in emails), AWS (hosting and storage), and Anthropic (identifies ingredients in fridge-scan photos). They process data on our behalf, not for their own use.

Deleting your data

Delete your account anytime in Settings → Delete account. It's a real deletion, not a deactivation:

  • Gone for good — your account, your cooks and ratings, your saved recipes and savings, and any messages or handles you've sent us are permanently removed from our systems.
  • Anonymous stats stay — counts like "a cook was completed" survive, but with your identity stripped out so they can't be tied back to you.
  • One anti-abuse safeguard stays — a scrambled, one-way code (not your email or name) that stops people from farming free unlocks with throwaway accounts. It holds no personal information, which is why anonymous usage limits may persist to prevent abuse.

You can also turn off camera access anytime in iOS Settings → Choppd — the app keeps working, just without the fridge scan. Prefer we handle a deletion for you? Email support@getchoppd.app.

Children

Choppd isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.
