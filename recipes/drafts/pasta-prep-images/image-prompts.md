**onepot-prep-1.webp — "Gather your equipment"**
Top-down staging shot: a large stainless pot with lid off, wooden spoon,
box grater, measuring cups, and a knife on a board — arranged together.
Staged state: "everything out before the heat goes on."

**onepot-prep-2.webp — "Measure your pasta"**
Top-down: dry short pasta (penne) filling a measuring cup on a kitchen
scale, the pasta box lying beside with its side panel facing up (weight
panel visible but unreadable — NO legible text). Judgment call: "measure
by the box's weight; a cup of dry short pasta looks like this."

**onepot-prep-3.webp — "Prepare your liquid" (REUSED by broth variants)**
Low 15° angle: a glass measuring jug of golden broth filled to a line,
standing next to the stovetop. Judgment call / staged state: "measured
and ready to pour — it goes in fast."
**onepot-prep-3b.webp — OPTIONAL bouillon variant**: the same jug of
plain water with bouillon cubes beside it on the counter. Same framing
as 3 (comparison-consistent).

**onepot-prep-4.webp — "Mince the garlic" (scale anchor required)**
Top-down: minced garlic on a cutting board, pieces uniformly tiny, with
a few grains of uncooked rice placed beside the pile as the size anchor
and the knife edge alongside. Judgment call: "pieces about the size of a
grain of rice."

**onepot-prep-5.webp — "Grate your cheese"**
Top-down: a mound of finely grated parmesan next to the remaining block
and a box grater edge. Judgment call: "grated from the block (not
pre-grated), this fine, this much relative to the block."

**onepot-prep-6.webp — "Measure your cream"**
Low 15° angle: heavy cream in a glass measuring cup filled to a line,
placed by the stove. Staged state: "measured, waiting by the stove for
the music phase."

**onepot-prep-7.webp — "Cut & season your chicken" (add-in only)**
Top-down: raw chicken cut into even one-inch pieces on a board, lightly
seasoned (visible pepper flecks acceptable — that IS the step), knife
alongside as scale anchor. 🚫 No garnish beyond the seasoning the step
names. ⚠️ Food-safety class: validate the raw-chicken appearance against
a real reference before shipping. Judgment call: "bite-size = this."
