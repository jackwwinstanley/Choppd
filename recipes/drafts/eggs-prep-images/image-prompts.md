**eggs-prep-1.webp — "Crack your eggs"**
Top-down: a hand cracking an egg on a FLAT counter surface (not a bowl
edge), a clear glass bowl beside with one egg already in, shells intact
and clean. Judgment call: "crack on the flat surface, not the edge."

**eggs-prep-2.webp — "Beat in the milk + salt"**
Top-down into the glass bowl: eggs beaten to one even pale-yellow colour
with a fork mid-whisk — NO streaks of white remaining, a few small
bubbles on top. Judgment call: "uniform yellow, no white streaks = beaten
enough; stop here."

**eggs-prep-3.webp — "Ready your pan, fat & spatula"**
Top-down staging shot: matte black nonstick pan (off-heat, on the
counter), rubber spatula, a small knob of butter on a dish, and a plate —
arranged within reach. Judgment call / staged state: "this is everything,
within arm's reach, before any heat."
