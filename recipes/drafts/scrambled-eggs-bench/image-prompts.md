# Image prompts — scrambled-eggs-bench (Cowork batch)

Base style (append to every prompt): *photorealistic food photography,
nonstick pan on a warm wood countertop, golden-hour side light, glossy
food-magazine finish, shallow depth of field, same kitchen and cookware in
every shot, wide landscape framing.*

---

**scrambled-eggs-bench/p1-c1.webp** — An empty nonstick pan sitting on a
lit burner, dial visibly turned to high, nothing in the pan — clean, dry
surface with a faint heat shimmer. (Pre-phase preheat step.)

**hero.jpg** — Plated soft scrambled eggs on a warm ceramic plate, creamy
pillowy curds glistening, fork resting beside, crack of black pepper on
top. (Finish shot — reused as the browse-card hero.)

**scrambled-eggs-bench/p2-c1.webp** — A knob of butter just melting and
foaming in a preheated nonstick pan, swirled to coat, gentle foam, no
browning.

**scrambled-eggs-bench/p2-c2.webp** — Pale whisked eggs being poured from
a small bowl into the buttered pan, pour caught mid-stream, surface still
undisturbed. 🚫 No garnish anywhere near the raw egg.

**scrambled-eggs-bench/p2-c3.webp** — The egg surface mostly glossy and
liquid but the EDGES turned solid white against the pan wall — the
white-rim moment, clearly visible contrast. (Doneness-gate reference:
must show exactly the state the gate copy describes.)

**scrambled-eggs-bench/p2-c4.webp** — A red rubber spatula mid-fold
dragging a slow figure-8 through partially set eggs, soft ribbons forming,
pan on the burner.

**scrambled-eggs-bench/p2-c5.webp** — Soft pillowy curds half-formed,
spatula folding them gently, still visibly moist between curds.

**scrambled-eggs-bench/p2-c6.webp** — Curds nearly done but distinctly
glossy and slightly wet on top — deliberately underdone-looking. No dry or
browned patches.

**scrambled-eggs-bench/p2-c7.webp** — THE OFF-HEAT SHOT: the pan sitting
on a folded kitchen towel on the counter, clearly AWAY from the stove, the
burner visible and empty in the background, one last fold in progress.
(Rule: off-heat must be visually unmistakable.)

**scrambled-eggs-bench/p2-c8.webp** — Close-up of the spatula lifting a
soft glossy fold of just-set eggs, creamy texture visible, no runny liquid
pooling. (Doneness-gate reference.)

**scrambled-eggs-bench/p2-c9.webp** — Eggs sliding from the pan onto the
warm plate, motion moment, pepper grinder standing by. 🚫 Nothing cut,
nothing garnished beyond the pepper.
